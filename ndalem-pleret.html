01 / THE PROBLEM

Renting customers from the platforms.

Like most small guest houses, Ndalem Pleret depended almost entirely on OTA platforms — Booking.com, Airbnb, and the like — to fill rooms. It worked, but it came at a cost:

  • A cut of every booking went to the platform as commission.
  • No direct relationship with guests — the platform owned the customer, the data, and the repeat business.
  • No channel of its own to send guests to, promote directly, or grow without paying a middleman.
"If you only sell through someone else's storefront, you don't own your business — you rent it."
02 / THE APPROACH

Give the business a storefront it controls.

The goal wasn't to abandon OTAs — they still bring reach. It was to add a direct channel the business fully owns, so any guest who finds it organically, by referral, or by returning books without the commission.

I designed the site mobile-first, because that's how guests in Indonesia actually browse and book — fast to load, easy to scan, and built around one clear action: check availability and reserve.

03 / WHAT I BUILT

A complete direct-booking flow.

  • Room browsing — clear photos, descriptions, and pricing so guests know exactly what they're booking.
  • Date & availability preview — guests check open dates before committing.
  • QRIS payment flow — instant, familiar local payment that closes the booking on the spot.
  • Mobile-first build — fast and frictionless on the phones guests actually use.
  • SEO foundations — so the guest house can be found directly, not only inside an OTA listing.
04 / THE OUTCOME

Live, owned, and commission-free.

The direct-booking channel is live and operational at ndalempleret.com. Every booking that comes through it saves the full OTA commission — and, just as importantly, the business now owns the guest relationship: the contact, the data, and the chance to earn repeat stays directly.

It's a foundation to build on — a channel the business controls and can grow, instead of one it rents.
