01 / THE PROBLEM

Repeat business, left on the table.

A carwash lives on customers coming back. But at Mbaki, retention depended entirely on someone remembering to do it by hand — and that almost never happens when you're busy running the place.

  • Follow-up was 100% manual — and manual means it mostly didn't happen.
  • No record of who came in, when they last visited, or who was due for another wash.
  • No trigger to reach out at the right moment — so loyal customers quietly drifted away.
"The cheapest customer to win is the one who already trusts you. Mbaki just had no system to bring them back."
02 / THE APPROACH

Make the system do the remembering.

Instead of asking the owner to add another task to a busy day, the answer was to take the task away entirely. I designed an automation that tracks every customer and handles outreach on its own — so retention happens whether or not anyone thinks about it.

I built it on n8n so it's visual, maintainable, and easy to extend later — with AI in the loop to keep messaging relevant rather than robotic.

03 / WHAT I BUILT

An end-to-end automation workflow.

  • Customer tracking — a running record of who visited and when, so nobody falls through the cracks.
  • Automated follow-up sequences — the right message, sent at the right interval, with no manual input.
  • Operational notifications — the business gets alerted to what matters, automatically.
  • AI-assisted messaging — outreach that reads like a person, not a template.
  • Built to scale — a foundation that holds up as the customer base grows.
04 / THE OUTCOME

Retention on autopilot.

The follow-up system now runs entirely on its own. Manual follow-up work has been eliminated, and the business has a scalable retention foundation — customers get reached at the right time, every time, without anyone lifting a finger.

It's the kind of quiet system that keeps working in the background while the owner focuses on running the business.
